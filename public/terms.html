2. Your Google account and data

  • You sign in with your own Google account, and your groups are stored as files in Google Drive. Your use of Google's services is also covered by Google's Terms of Service.
  • You're responsible for the data you add and for who you share groups with. Invite links let anyone who has them read and edit a group, so share them carefully.
  • Keep backups of anything important. Deleting a group's sheet in Google Drive deletes the group.

3. Not a payment or financial service

  • SpreadShare only records amounts. It doesn't hold, send or receive money. "Pay with UPI" just opens your own UPI app, and any payment is between you and the other person, under your bank's and UPI app's terms.
  • Balances, statements, budgets, forecasts and insights are for your information only, not financial, tax or legal advice.

4. Check what the app fills in

Receipt scanning, imports from screenshots, statements, CSV files and messages, automatic categories and exchange rates are best-effort and can be wrong. Please check amounts, dates and splits before you save them.
