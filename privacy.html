Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

In short: SpreadShare has no server and no database of its own. Your groups are Google Sheets in your own Google Drive, and the app talks to Google directly from your device. We don't collect, store, sell or share your data, and there are no ads, analytics or trackers.

Who we are

SpreadShare is a free, open-source app for splitting expenses with friends and groups, made by Sarin C Jacob. The source code is public at github.com/Sarin-jacob/SpreadShare, so you can check everything this policy says.

What the app asks Google for, and why

When you sign in with Google, SpreadShare asks for these permissions:

  • Your name, email address and profile picture: to show who added what, and to identify you in the groups you join.
  • Google Sheets: each group is a spreadsheet. The app creates group spreadsheets, adds entries to them and reads the groups you're a member of. It doesn't open or change any other spreadsheet in your account.
  • Google Drive, only files SpreadShare creates or opens: a SpreadShare_Workspaces folder, a small file listing your groups, a file with your preferences (budgets, your UPI ID), and receipt photos you attach. The app can't see your other Drive files.

You can see or remove this access at any time in your Google Account under Third-party apps & services.

Where your data lives

  • In your Google Drive. Everything you enter in a group (expenses, payments, comments, notes, members' names, UPI IDs you choose to share) is stored in that group's Google Sheet, owned by whoever created the group.
  • On your device. A copy of your groups is kept in the browser's storage so the app works offline, along with your settings, a sign-in token that expires after an hour, and (if you download it) the receipt reader. Signing out removes this copy.
  • Nowhere else. We have no server, so we never receive your data.

Who can see your data

  • Members of your group see everything in that group.
  • Invite links: when you invite someone, the group's sheet is shared as "anyone with the link can edit", so they can open it. Anyone you give the link to (or who gets it from them) can read and edit the group. Only share invite links with people you trust, and you can turn link sharing off in Google Drive.
  • Receipt photos you attach are uploaded to your Drive and shared as "anyone with the link can view", so other group members can see them.
  • Google stores your files under its own privacy policy.

Processed on your device only

Receipt photos, PDF bills, screenshots of payment history, bank statements, CSV files and pasted bank or UPI messages are read by the app on your device. They are never sent to us or to any AI service. Automatic categories are worked out on your device from your own past entries. The app lock (fingerprint, face or device PIN) uses your device's own unlock; nothing about it leaves your device.

Other services the app contacts

Your device connects directly to these services. None of them receive your expenses or personal details from SpreadShare:

  • Google (accounts.google.com, googleapis.com): sign-in and your Sheets / Drive files.
  • GitHub Pages: serves the app's files. GitHub may log your IP address, like any website host (GitHub's privacy statement).
  • jsDelivr (cdn.jsdelivr.net) and PaddlePaddle's model storage (paddle-model-ecology.bj.bcebos.com): download the receipt reader's code and models, once. Only the files are requested; your receipts are not sent.
  • ExchangeRate-API (open.er-api.com): today's exchange rates when you enter an amount in another currency. Only the currency is sent.
  • Your UPI app: "Pay with UPI" opens it with the payee and amount filled in. SpreadShare never sees the payment.

Google API Services

SpreadShare's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data from Google is only used to provide the app's features to you, is not transferred to anyone, is not used for advertising, and is never read by people.

Deleting your data

  • Sign out (Settings) to remove everything stored on your device.
  • Delete a group's sheet, or the whole SpreadShare_Workspaces folder, in Google Drive to delete it for good. Groups owned by someone else can only be deleted by their owner.
  • Remove SpreadShare's access in your Google Account.

Children

SpreadShare is not meant for children under 13 and doesn't knowingly handle their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. The full history is in the public code repository.
